01 About summary

I'm a DevOps and Production Support Engineer with 5+ years operating large-scale telecom platforms across AWS, Azure, and on-premises Linux environments. I've spent most of my career keeping high-volume production systems inside SLA — monitoring, incident response, root cause analysis, and the CI/CD automation that prevents the next incident from happening.

At Amdocs, I supported the migration of AT&T's Kafka-based mediation platform — 200M+ billable records processed per day — from on-premises to Azure Kubernetes Service.
